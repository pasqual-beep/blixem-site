How it works

Clamp. Punch. Carry on.

    Clamp it on

Questions

Good questions

R1,499 for a boxing bag?

It's a full kit, not just a bag: a boxing bag on a spring, an arm and a clamp made for golf-cart roof posts, plus a pump and the BliXem box. That's about the price of a dozen premium golf balls, and you won't lose this one in the dam. You pay nothing until yours is ready to ship.

Will it fit my cart?

It's made for the front roof post. The clamp is made for posts of about 19–35 mm, and we're testing it on Club Car and E-Z-GO carts before launch. Not sure about yours? WhatsApp us a photo of the post. If it doesn't fit when it arrives, send it back within 7 days for a refund.

Will it scratch or damage the cart?

It tightens by hand and comes off in seconds. We're testing it for marks on painted and powder-coated posts, and we'll tell you what we find before you pay.

Is it allowed at my club? What about hire carts?

On your own cart, it's your call. On a hire cart, ask the starter or pro shop first; some clubs have rules about fitting things to their carts. It clamps on by hand and comes off in seconds, so it goes home with you.

Is it safe?

Only use BliXem when the cart is parked. Never while driving, and keep it clear of anyone getting in or out.

Is it built to last, or just a joke gift?

The joke is the name. The kit is made to be hit every round, and we're testing samples for strength before we order. If anything is faulty, you have 6 months to send it back.

What if they don't like it?

Send it back within 7 days of delivery for a refund, as long as it isn't personalised. You only pay the courier to return it. Personalised BliXems are made just for you, so we can only take them back if they're faulty.

When will I get it? Will it come before Father's Day?

Our first batch is planned for before Father's Day, Sunday 20 June 2027. We'll keep you posted along the way. You only pay when yours is ready to ship, so if it can't reach you in time, you'll know before you've paid a cent.

Why reserve? What's the catch?

No catch. Reserving costs nothing and needs no card. It holds your place in the first batch and tells us how many to order. Reservations are filled in the order they come in. When yours is ready, we send a payment link. Don't want it anymore? Ignore the link or tell us.

How do I pay?

When your BliXem is ready, we send a secure payment link by WhatsApp and email. You can pay by card or instant EFT.

What does delivery cost?

Delivery is extra: from about R70 to a PUDO locker near you, or about R120 to your door, anywhere in South Africa. You'll see the exact cost before you pay.

Can I put a name, a face or a logo on the bag? What does that cost?

Yes. We print names, logos, faces and funny designs on the BliXem bag. Personalising costs extra; we confirm the price before you pay. We only print someone's face with their OK, and we can say no to anything hurtful or rude.

Can I send it straight to the person it's for?

Yes. Add their name and address when you pay, and we'll leave the price out of the box.

Do you do bulk or trade prices?

Yes. Golf days and companies get bulk pricing from 10 bags, pro shops and resellers get trade pricing with no minimum, and wholesale pricing starts at 100 bags. Ask for a quote.

What does "BliXem" mean?

It's our take on the Afrikaans word for lightning. It's also what most golfers say after a bad shot.

Who's behind it

Dale and Pasqual, the unlikely brothers

We're Dale and Pasqual, two unlikely brothers who married twins. Somewhere between the family braais and more in-law jokes than we can count, we found the thing we share most: a love of golf. Not always good golf. Just a lot of it, with plenty of bad shots along the way.

BliXem started the way the best ideas do: on a golf cart, after a bad hole, with one of us saying "bliksem!" a bit too loudly. We wanted something to hit that wasn't the 7-iron, the cart or each other. So we're making it.

BliXem is for every round that doesn't go to plan, and for the people you play it with.
