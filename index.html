How it works

Clamp. Punch. Carry on.

  1. 1

    Clamp it on

    Fit the clamp to the front roof post of your golf cart and tighten it by hand.

  2. 2

    Punch

    Three-putt? Lost ball in the water? Let the bag have it. Then breathe, and play the next shot.

  3. 3

    Unclamp in seconds

    Done for the day? It comes off in seconds, so it goes home with you and not with the cart.

Questions

Good questions

Will it fit my Club Car or E-Z-GO?

We're choosing a clamp made for most cart roof posts (about 19–35 mm), and we're testing it on Club Car and E-Z-GO carts before launch.

Is it safe?

Only use BliXem when the cart is parked. Never while driving, and keep it clear of passengers getting in and out.

Can I put a name, a face or my logo on it?

Yes. Names and logos go on the ball, faces and funny designs are printed in full colour on the bag, and you can add a message on the gift box. Personalised BliXems are made to order. We only print someone's face with their OK.

Do you do bulk or trade prices?

Yes. Golf days and companies get bulk pricing from 10 bags, pro shops and resellers get trade pricing with no minimum, and wholesale pricing starts at 100 bags. Every order gets a written quote. Ask for a quote.

Can a golf course fit one to every hire cart?

Yes, that's what it's made for. It clamps on by hand and comes off in seconds. Ask us about fleet pricing and your club's crest on the ball.

Do you deliver to my area?

Yes. We deliver anywhere in South Africa.

When can I get one?

We're planning the first batch for before Father's Day 2027 (Sunday 20 June). Sign up above and we'll tell you as soon as you can order.

How much is it?

The launch price is R999. Signing up is free and you don't pay anything now.

What does "BliXem" mean?

It's our take on the Afrikaans word for lightning. It's also what most golfers say after a bad shot.
