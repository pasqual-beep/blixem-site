Privacy

This page explains what happens to the details you give us on blixemgolf.co.za or on WhatsApp. We follow South Africa's Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA).

Who we are

BliXem is a brand of Thriamvos (Pty) Ltd, a South African company. Contact us at hello@blixemgolf.co.za or on WhatsApp at 066 255 0508.

What we collect

  • When you reserve: your name, WhatsApp number, email address and how many you'd like. If you choose to tell us, also what it's for and whether you'd like it personalised.
  • When you ask for a bulk quote: your name, company or club, email, phone number if you give it, what you're buying for, how many, the date you need them and any message you add.
  • Where you came from: the campaign tag on the link you used, or the website that sent you (for example "instagram"). Never anything that identifies you.
  • Your news choice: whether you ticked the box to get BliXem news.
  • Messages you send us on WhatsApp or by email.

Why we collect it

To hold your reservation, to tell you when BliXem is ready, to send you a payment link when yours is ready to ship, and to reply about quotes you asked for. These messages are about your reservation or quote, not marketing.

We'll only send you other news and offers if you ticked the box saying yes. You can say stop at any time, by replying "STOP" or emailing us, and we keep a list so we never contact you again after you opt out. We don't sell or rent your details to anyone, and we don't add you to other mailing lists.

Do you have to give it?

We need your name, WhatsApp number and email to hold a reservation and send your payment link. Everything else is optional.

Where it's kept and who sees it

What you send through the website is saved in a Google Form and Google Sheet in our own Google account, which only we can see. If you message us on WhatsApp, the chat is handled by WhatsApp (Meta). Google and WhatsApp may store it on servers outside South Africa, under their own data-protection terms.

How long we keep it

Reservations and quotes: until you ask us to remove them, or until 12 months after launch if you haven't bought or replied, whichever comes first.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your details, or object to us using them, at any time. You can unsubscribe from any message we send. Email hello@blixemgolf.co.za.

If you're unhappy with how we handled your details, you can complain to the Information Regulator: Woodmead North Office Park, 54 Maxwell Drive, Woodmead, Johannesburg, 2191; POPIAComplaints@inforegulator.org.za; 010 023 5200; inforegulator.org.za.
