Privacy

This page explains what happens to the details you give us on blixemgolf.co.za. We follow South Africa's Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA).

Who we are

BliXem is a brand of Thriamvos (Pty) Ltd, a South African company. Contact us at hello@blixemgolf.co.za.

What we collect

  • Your name and email address.
  • Your WhatsApp number, only if you choose to give it.
  • Your company or golf day details, only if you choose to give them.
  • Whether you'd like to pre-order.

Why we collect it

Only to tell you when BliXem is available, and to reply about pre-orders or golf days you asked about. We don't sell or rent your details to anyone, and we don't add you to other mailing lists.

How long we keep it

Until you ask us to remove it, or until 12 months after launch if you haven't bought or replied, whichever comes first.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your details at any time, and you can unsubscribe from any message we send. Email hello@blixemgolf.co.za. If you're unhappy with how we handled your details, you can complain to the Information Regulator (inforegulator.org.za).
